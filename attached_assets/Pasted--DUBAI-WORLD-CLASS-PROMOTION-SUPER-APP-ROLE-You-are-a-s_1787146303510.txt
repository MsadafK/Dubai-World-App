# 🇦🇪 DUBAI — WORLD-CLASS PROMOTION SUPER-APP

## ROLE

You are a **senior product architect, UX/UI designer, frontend engineer, backend engineer, database architect, and QA engineer**.

Your task is to design and build a **world-class Dubai promotion and discovery platform**.

This is NOT a basic tourism website.

The final product should feel like a premium combination of:

* Visit Dubai
* Airbnb
* Booking.com
* Tripadvisor
* Groupon
* Google Maps
* A luxury lifestyle platform
* A business marketplace
* A lead-generation platform

But DO NOT copy their branding, layouts, or UI. Create a completely original product identity.

### PRODUCT VISION

The goal is to create a digital platform so impressive, polished, immersive, useful, and premium that a Dubai tourism authority, major Dubai business, investor, or government stakeholder could look at it and think:

**"Wow. This is how Dubai should be represented digitally."**

The product should communicate:

**Luxury + Innovation + Discovery + Business + Entertainment + Hospitality + Future**
